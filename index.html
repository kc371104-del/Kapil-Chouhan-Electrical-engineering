About Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering is a branch of engineering that deals with electricity, electrical machines, circuits, power systems and electronic equipment.

Important Subjects

1. Physics

Study of physical quantities, motion, force and energy.

2. IT System

Study of computers, internet, HTML and digital technology.

3. Electrical Fundamentals

Study of voltage, current, resistance and electrical circuits.

4. Engineering Mechanics

Study of force, friction, motion and equilibrium.

Basic Electrical Formulas

Ohm's Law: V = I × R

Power: P = V × I

Current: I = V / R
